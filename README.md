# APA 7 Referencing Checklist

An interactive checklist for reviewing citations and reference lists against the *Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association* (7th ed.).

**Use it online:** [is.gd/apa7checklist](https://is.gd/apa7checklist)  
Full address: https://ramin-shafieian.github.io/apa7-checklist/

- Tick items as you review your draft; progress is saved automatically in your browser.
- Section links and per-section progress on wide screens.
- Questions or corrections? Use the **Contact me** link at the bottom of the page.

This is an independent study aid and is not produced or endorsed by the American Psychological Association. Follow the Manual and your instructor's or journal's requirements where they differ.

Compiled by Ramin Shafieian with AI assistance.
